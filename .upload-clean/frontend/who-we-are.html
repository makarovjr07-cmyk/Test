Our School Community

Kankai Secondary School in Mai Nagarpalika-4, Danabari, Sitali, Ilam is a community founded school that believes every child can learn with care, discipline, and opportunity. Established in 2017 B.S., the school serves families from the surrounding neighborhoods and villages and keeps strong ties with parents and local leaders. Our daily routines balance academics, character, and respect so students feel safe and motivated. We aim to be a trusted place where children grow in knowledge and confidence.

We focus on strong foundations in language, mathematics, science, and digital literacy. From early grades to secondary, students are guided to ask questions, practice problem solving, and build clear communication. Practical learning is important to us, so lessons connect to real life and community needs. This helps students understand not just what to learn, but why it matters.

Our school culture values punctuality, cleanliness, and mutual respect. Teachers and staff model supportive behavior and encourage students to help one another. Parents are invited to share feedback through meetings and visits so we can keep improving. We believe learning works best when the whole community is involved.

Facilities such as the library, computer lab, science lab, and safe play areas support active learning. We also run Computer Engineering classes for grades 9 to 12 to build job ready skills. Co curricular activities, sports, and ECA programs help students discover talents beyond the classroom. This balanced approach prepares students for further study and responsible citizenship.

Above all, Kankai Secondary School is committed to the growth of each student. We guide learners to set goals, practice discipline, and take pride in their progress. Our teachers invest time in mentoring and individual support. We welcome families who want a school that combines academic strength with heart and community values.
