Cafeteria and Student Nutrition

Our cafeteria provides a clean and friendly space where students can enjoy nutritious meals. We understand that good food supports good learning, so hygiene and quality are our top priorities. Meals are prepared with care and served in a safe environment. Students are encouraged to eat balanced foods that support healthy growth.

The menu includes local and familiar dishes that students enjoy. We aim to provide energy for a full day of learning and activities. Drinking water and basic nutrition guidance are part of our daily routine. The cafeteria team maintains cleanliness of tables, floors, and utensils.

We teach students to respect shared spaces by cleaning up after themselves and keeping the cafeteria tidy. These habits build responsibility and discipline. Teachers and staff supervise meal times to ensure safety and order. A calm meal environment helps students return to class focused and refreshed.

Parents and guardians are welcome to share feedback on student nutrition. We review suggestions and make improvements when possible. The goal is to support health while keeping meals affordable and accessible. This partnership helps us serve students better.

The cafeteria is part of our broader effort to care for the whole child. By promoting healthy eating and good habits, we strengthen both academic and personal development. A well fed student is ready to learn and participate fully. This is our commitment at Kankai Secondary School.
