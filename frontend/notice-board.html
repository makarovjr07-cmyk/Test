Welcome to Shree Kankai Secondary School

Shree Kankai Secondary School was established in 2017 B.S. and is located in the heart of Mai Nagarpalika-4, Danabari, Sitali, Ilam.

We have a young, energetic, dedicated and dynamic team of teachers to provide quality education based on practical and technology-focused learning.

We are running Computer Engineering classes from Class 9 to 12 with the aim of producing job-oriented and skillful students.

Message From the Principal

Dear Students / Parents and Guardians,

It is my great pleasure to introduce Shree Kankai Secondary School Mai Nagarpalika-4, Danabari, Sitali, Ilam as a well-recognized community school of Ilam. It was founded in 2017 B.S and has managed to stand out among a group of high-quality education providers in the country.

This school, which is always motivated by a noble vision, aspires to make a significant contribution to nation-building. This school has provided time-tested instruction to all of its pupils since the beginning of its existence. It provides a vast array of options, both within and outside of our taught curriculum. This is a proven school with a positive attitude and a belief that anything is possible.
