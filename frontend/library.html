Library and Reading Culture

The school library is a quiet, welcoming space that supports study and curiosity. It offers a growing collection of textbooks, reference materials, and general reading books. Students are encouraged to borrow books regularly and build a reading habit. The library is open during school hours for guided use.

Reading helps students improve language, imagination, and critical thinking. Teachers assign reading tasks and guide students to explore topics beyond the classroom. The library supports research for projects and helps students practice independent learning. This builds confidence and academic strength.

We also promote a reading culture through book talks, reading challenges, and class visits. These activities make reading enjoyable and social. Students learn to share ideas and discuss what they read. This improves communication skills and respect for diverse views.

The library staff ensures books are organized and cared for properly. Students are taught how to handle books responsibly and keep the space tidy. Quiet rules and study zones allow focused learning for all. A peaceful library helps students concentrate and reflect.

As the school grows, we continue adding new books and resources. We welcome community support to expand the collection. The library remains a key part of our mission to build lifelong learners. It is a place where knowledge and curiosity meet.
