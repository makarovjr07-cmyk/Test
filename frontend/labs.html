Science, Computer, and Electronics Labs

Practical learning is essential at Kankai Secondary School, and our labs make this possible. We maintain computer, science, and electronics labs where students can apply what they learn in class. These spaces help students develop hands on skills and deeper understanding. Safety and discipline are always emphasized.

In the science lab, students perform experiments that bring concepts to life. They learn observation, measurement, and careful reporting. Teachers guide activities step by step so students gain confidence and accuracy. This builds a strong foundation for higher studies in science.

The computer lab supports digital literacy and technical skill development. Students practice typing, basic programming, and project work. Our Computer Engineering program for grades 9 to 12 uses this lab for advanced learning. These experiences prepare students for modern opportunities.

The electronics lab introduces students to circuits, tools, and practical troubleshooting. They learn how technology works and how to approach problems logically. Projects encourage teamwork and creativity while reinforcing safety habits. This makes learning active and meaningful.

We continue upgrading our labs to keep them useful and relevant. Teachers plan practical sessions that connect directly to curriculum goals. Students gain skills that go beyond exams and support real world success. These labs are a proud part of our school.
