First Aid and Student Wellbeing

Student safety and wellbeing are priorities for our school. We maintain first aid support to respond quickly to minor injuries or health concerns. Basic supplies are available on campus, and staff are trained to give initial care. This helps students feel safe and supported.

Teachers and staff monitor student health throughout the day. When a student feels unwell, we provide rest, basic care, and communication with parents. Clear procedures guide how we handle emergencies and referrals. This ensures students receive help promptly.

We also promote healthy habits through cleanliness, hydration, and personal hygiene. Students learn the importance of hand washing and maintaining a clean environment. These habits reduce illness and create a healthier school community. Health education is part of our broader learning goals.

The school collaborates with local health services when needed. We invite health professionals for awareness programs and check ups when possible. This partnership strengthens our ability to care for students. It also teaches students about preventive health.

First aid is not only about emergencies; it is about building a safe and caring environment. By being prepared, we protect learning time and student confidence. Families can trust that their children are cared for while at school. This is a core part of our responsibility.
