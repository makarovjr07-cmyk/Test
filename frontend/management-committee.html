School Management Committee

The School Management Committee provides overall guidance and governance for Kankai Secondary School. It includes representatives from the community who care deeply about education and student welfare. The committee works to keep the school aligned with its mission and values. Their leadership ensures steady growth and responsible decision making.

Committee members support policy development, planning, and oversight of major initiatives. They help improve infrastructure, learning resources, and student services. Regular meetings allow the committee to review progress and address challenges. This collaborative approach strengthens the school each year.

Transparency and accountability are important to the committee. Budgets and resource allocation are planned carefully so that students benefit directly. The committee also monitors safety standards, discipline systems, and overall school performance. These efforts keep the school stable and focused on learning.

The committee encourages strong partnerships between the school and the community. Parents and local leaders are invited to share ideas and participate in school events. This connection builds trust and shared ownership of the school's success. The committee listens to feedback and uses it to guide improvements.

With a clear vision and a service mindset, the School Management Committee supports the long term future of Kankai Secondary School. Their commitment helps teachers teach well and students learn well. We are grateful for their time, guidance, and dedication. Together we continue building a stronger school.
